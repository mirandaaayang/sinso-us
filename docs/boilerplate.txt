300-WORD BOILERPLATE (for press releases and longer pitches)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sinso is the US-facing brand of Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. (新兆印业),
a color packaging manufacturer founded in 1998 with more than 25 years of
production experience and a factory spanning over 15,000 square meters in
the Fenghua High-tech Development Zone in Ningbo, Zhejiang.

What separates Sinso from conventional Extended Color Gamut (ECG) printers
is its workflow architecture. Most ECG presses add orange, green, and violet
inks on top of a four-color CMYK base, reaching approximately 72% of the
visible color gamut. Sinso's Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 eight-color press
runs a native RGB workflow: designer files in RGB are converted directly to
a seven-ink set, and CMYK is never laid down as a base. This approach
reproduces approximately 85% of the visible gamut and eliminates the muddy
color shifts that occur in saturated reds, oranges, greens, and blues when
those colors are built on top of CMYK.

Sinso is G7 Master Printer certified, the Idealliance process-control
standard for accurate gray balance and tonal consistency. The company is
also GMI (Graphic Measures International) certified — qualifying it as an
approved vendor for Target, Walmart, and Costco private-label packaging
programs. Sinso serves three US-facing verticals: Artist Editions (jigsaw
puzzles, art books, museum and gallery licensed reproductions), Toy and
Game Packaging, and Beauty and Personal Care Packaging. A US operations
team coordinates project management, color approval, and logistics for
North American brands. Named customers include In Joy Puzzle Company
(Brooklyn, New York), which collaborated with artist Holton Rower on a
jigsaw puzzle line printed in RGB.

Sinso operates two web properties: sinso.us for US-facing customers and
sinsoprinting.com for global accounts.
