FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Technology

What is ECG printing?

ECG stands for Extended Color Gamut printing. It uses more than the four standard CMYK inks — usually adding orange, green, and violet — so the press can reproduce colors that fall outside what CMYK can reach.

How is Sinso's ECG different from typical ECG printing?

Most ECG presses lay OGV inks on top of a CMYK base layer. Sinso runs a native RGB workflow on a Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 — there is no CMYK base. That difference is why we reach roughly 85% of the visible color gamut versus ~72% for typical hybrid ECG.

What press do you run?

A Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 in 8-color configuration, with inline coater.

Can you handle the artwork conversion to RGB-native separations?

Yes. Most artwork arrives in either RGB or CMYK. Our prepress team generates the native RGB separations for ECG jobs as part of the standard prepress workflow.

Certifications and capabilities

Are you G7 certified?

Yes. Sinso is a G7 Master Printer, certified through Idealliance. G7 governs how our proofs are calibrated to match the press.

Are you GMI certified?

Yes. GMI qualifies our factory for the approved vendor lists at major US retailers including Target, Walmart, and Costco.

What kind of packaging does Sinso make?

Folding cartons, rigid set-up boxes, magnetic-closure gift boxes, blister and display backers. We focus on three verticals: artist edition packaging, toy and game packaging, and beauty and personal care packaging.

What don't you do?

Flexible packaging like pouches, films, and flow wrap. Corrugated shipper boxes (we partner for outer master cartons). US-based fulfillment and warehousing.

What substrates can you print on?

SBS, FBB, kraft, recycled board, and specialty coated stocks. If you have a specific spec in mind, send it with the brief.

Project mechanics

7 to 10 days from receipt of files and brief. Samples are airshipped to the US.

How does the project flow work?

Brief and file submission → physical sample airshipped in 7–10 days → G7-calibrated digital proof reviewed and approved → production press run with inline color measurement → delivery FOB Ningbo, or DDP on request.

Do you publish pricing?
