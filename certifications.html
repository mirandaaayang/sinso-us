Certifications

Credentials

One credential per section. Each shows what it covers, who issued it, what it lets a buyer do, and how to verify it.

G7 Master Printer

Idealliance. Proof-to-press color conformance for gray balance and tonal reproduction — the production standard that ensures an approved proof matches the press run on every job and every reorder.

Verify: Idealliance G7 Master Printer program.

GMI

Graphic Measures International. Qualifies Sinso as an approved packaging vendor for US retailer programs including Target, Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Academy Sports, and Walgreens. Buyers supplying these retailers can onboard Sinso without a separate vendor qualification process.

Verify: Audited; certificate on file, available on request.

ISO 9001:2015

Bureau Veritas. Quality management system certification. Scope: printing of paper products (packaging color boxes, self-adhesive labels, and paper cards). Buyers can cite the certificate number in vendor qualification documentation. Legal entity: Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd.

Verify: Certificate no. CN09/21763, valid to 2027-11-16.

ISO 14001

Environmental management system certification. Independent third-party verification that Sinso's factory-level environmental management practices meet the ISO 14001 standard.

Verify: Audited; certificate on file, available on request.

FSC Chain of Custody

Bureau Veritas, standard FSC-STD-40-004 V3-1. Covers FSC 100%, FSC Mix, and FSC Recycled materials. Buyers can specify FSC-labeled substrates and receive chain-of-custody documentation for their own sustainability reporting. Legal entity: Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd.

Verify: Certificate code BV-COC-129082 — info.fsc.org.

SMETA / Sedex

Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit. 4-pillar social and ethical audit covering labor standards, health and safety, environment, and business ethics. Required by many enterprise retailers and brand licensors for approved-supplier onboarding.

Verify: Audited; report on file, available on request.

Disney FAMA

The Walt Disney Company — Facility and Merchandise Authorization. Qualifies Sinso as an authorized production facility for Disney-licensed products. Buyers licensing Disney IP can onboard Sinso without a separate facility assessment.

Verify: Audited; authorization on file, available on request.

CTV ECG Certification

Independent third-party validation of Sinso's 7-color Expanded Color Gamut printing process. Substantiates the ~80% Pantone coverage within ΔE 2.5 measured across production runs — the benchmark cited on our technology page is backed by this external certification, not self-asserted.

Verify: CTV certification on file, available on request.

Process patents

Holds 25 process patents.

Product compliance testing and documentation (heavy-metals / transit / ink safety) available on request.
