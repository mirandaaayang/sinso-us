Industries

Three verticals where ECG color is part of the product

Sinso is not a general-purpose printer chasing every category. We focus on three verticals where the color on the box is inseparable from the value of the product inside.

Vertical 01

Artist Editions

Puzzle, game, and collector packaging where the box reproduces an artist's original. Deep violets, saturated oranges, and continuous gradients that CMYK flattens.

Vertical 02

Toys & Games

Retail toy boxes, rigid gift sets, display and backer cards. Built to Target, Walmart, and specialty toy retailer vendor specs, with GMI certification already in place.

Vertical 03

Beauty & Personal Care

Soft-touch laminated cartons, embossed rigid set-up boxes, and hot-foil beauty sets. For brands where the unboxing finish is part of the brand promise.
