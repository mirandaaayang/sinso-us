Print What You Actually Designed

Most packaging prints darker, duller, and less accurate than the design file. That's not a calibration issue — it's a structural limitation of standard 4-color CMYK printing. Sinso solves this at the press level.

We run 7-color ECG (Expanded Color Gamut) printing — CMYK plus Orange, Green, and Violet ink channels — as our base process. The design target is 95%+ Pantone coverage and ΔE < 2 color accuracy; current measured performance across recent production runs is approximately 80% of Pantone within ΔE 2.5. For work where color is load-bearing, it's the structural fix. For commodity print, standard CMYK is sufficient.

Standard CMYK vs. Sinso 7-Color ECG

Standard CMYK Sinso 7-Color ECG
Ink channels 4 7
Pantone coverage ~60% 95%+ (design target; ~80% measured)
Color accuracy (ΔE) Variable, often >4 ΔE < 2 (design target; ~2.5 measured)
Screen-to-print shift High on saturated colors Minimal
Spot color add-on Required per color Not needed
