About Sinso

Sinso is the US-facing brand of Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. (新兆印业), a color packaging manufacturer founded in 1998 in Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. The company has operated in color packaging for more than 25 years and produces from a facility spanning over 15,000 square meters in the Fenghua High-tech Development Zone.

What makes Sinso different

Sinso runs 7-color ECG (Expanded Color Gamut) printing as its base process — CMYK plus Orange, Green, and Violet ink channels on a Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 (8-color, inline coater). Measured across production runs: approximately 80% of Pantone within ΔE 2.5, independently validated by CTV ECG certification. See the full technical explanation on the Technology page.

Standard CMYK printing covers approximately 60% of Pantone. The ECG difference is most visible in licensed artwork reproduction, saturated brand colors, and Pantone spot-color jobs that would otherwise require additional spot ink plates.

Facility and equipment

Sinso's factory is located at No. 118 Huiming Road, Fenghua High-tech Development Zone, Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. The site covers more than 12,000 square meters with over 15,000 square meters of production area. The primary press is a Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 configured for eight colors. The facility runs prepress, press, postpress, and finishing in-house under unified color management.

Certifications

Sinso holds: G7 Master Printer · GMI · ISO 9001:2015 (CN09/21763) · ISO 14001 · FSC Chain of Custody (BV-COC-129082) · SMETA · Disney FAMA · CTV ECG. Registry codes and verification contacts for each are on the Certifications page.

What Sinso prints

Sinso's US operation focuses on three verticals:

  • Artist & Collector Editions — jigsaw puzzles, art books, museum and gallery licensed reproductions, limited-edition prints
  • Games & Puzzles — puzzle boxes, board game boxes, collectible card and game packaging
  • Retail Packaging — toy lines, gift sets, beauty and personal care secondary packaging

The global operation also produces rigid boxes, folding cartons, paper cards, food-safe boxes, and RFID-enabled labels through sinsoprinting.com.

US operations

A US-based operations team handles project management, color approval, sample coordination, and logistics for North American brands. The US team is the primary point of contact for new accounts and is reachable at hello@sinso.us.

Named collaborations

In Joy Puzzle Company, a Brooklyn-based puzzle publisher, collaborated with Sinso on a jigsaw puzzle line featuring artwork by artist Holton Rower. The collaboration used Sinso's 7-color ECG workflow to reproduce the artist's saturated color palette in process printing.
