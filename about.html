About Sinso

Sinso is the US-facing brand of Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. (新兆印业), a color packaging manufacturer founded in 1998 in Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. The company has operated in color packaging for more than 25 years and produces from a facility spanning over 15,000 square meters in the Fenghua High-tech Development Zone.

Company history

2022
Installed the Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 eight-color press — entering 7-color ECG printing with ultra-wide color gamut capability, independently validated by CTV ECG certification.
2021
Built an in-house APS+MES production management system to drive company-wide scheduling and quality tracking.
2015–2020
Introduced a spot-color automatic ink-matching system for standardized color control; upgraded back-end finishing equipment; added Disney, Academy, and Home Depot as designated packaging and printing suppliers.
2011–2014
Added Heidelberg XL 75 and XL 105 presses and an automatic laminating machine; adopted advanced color management; achieved GMI certification; became a designated supplier for Target, Best Buy, Lowe's, Petco, Wilko, and Walgreens.
2001–2010
Introduced CTP publishing, a Heidelberg CD102 five-color press, automatic die-cutting, and folder-gluer; started supply relationships with Walmart and Target.
1998
Founded in Ningbo with a Heidelberg CD74 four-color press.
