Plastic-free options

Most toy packaging constructions can move to plastic-free or significantly reduced-plastic designs without a structural compromise.

What can be replaced:

  • PET windows - paper windows in most folding carton constructions
  • Thermoformed PET inserts - paperboard tray-and-sleeve or molded-pulp trays
  • Standard PET blister - paper-backed paperboard backer cards with no blister

What's harder to replace:

  • Deep-draw blister shapes that hold irregular toy forms - these require thermoforming for now, though tray-and-sleeve is an option in most cases
  • Clamshells securing high-value items where theft resistance is required

FSC-certified board

All paper-based stock is available in FSC-certified grades. FSC chain of custody number: BV-COC-129082 - verifiable at info.fsc.org.

EU PPWR readiness

Europe's new Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) sets recycled-content and recyclability requirements phasing in from 2030. Sinso's paperboard-based constructions are designed to meet these requirements. See the PPWR compliance guide.

When ECG is worth it for toy packaging

7-color ECG adds Orange, Green, and Violet to CMYK - covering 82% of Pantone within ΔE 2.5. For toy packaging, ECG pays when:

  • Signature character colors - licensed orange, green, teal, or violet that must match the approved Pantone exactly
  • Lifestyle photography on packaging where skin tones, sky gradients, and foliage need to hold true
  • Side-by-side SKUs where color variation across reorders is visible at retail
  • Saturated fills and gradients in character illustration that CMYK shifts or bands

Standard CMYK is sufficient when the packaging is primarily text, neutrals, and simple flat graphics. Both processes run on the same press and G7-calibrated workflow.

Toy packaging FAQ

Do you make the toys too?

We focus on the packaging. Printing, finishing, and converting run in our own Ningbo factory. For other components your program needs - such as shipper cartons - we have a robust local supply chain. Any partner-supplied components are identified on the quote.

What is your MOQ for toy packaging?

MOQ depends on structure, substrate, and finishing. Contact hello@sinso.us with your dieline and spec - we respond within 24 hours.

What is your production lead time after proof approval?

Physical samples are airshipped in 7-10 days from file receipt. Production lead time after proof approval depends on structure, run length, and finishing operations. Contact us with your spec for a firm timeline.

Can you quote itemized by component?

Yes. Quotes include substrate, coating, ink configuration, board weight, adhesive and fastener specs as separate line items. Contact hello@sinso.us with your dieline or spec sheet.

Are you Disney FAMA authorized? What does it cover?

Yes - Sinso is Disney FAMA (Facility and Merchandise Authorization) authorized. For specific scope and current authorization details, contact hello@sinso.us.

Can you ship packaging to our toy factory in Guangdong or Vietnam?

Contact us with your factory address and program details - we can quote shipping to assembly sites. Reach us at hello@sinso.us.

What compliance documentation do you provide?

Heavy-metals testing, transit testing documentation, and ink-safety documentation are available on request. Audit reports (SMETA / Sedex) and all certification documents are available to qualified buyers on request. See the full list at certifications.

Can you replace plastic blisters and windows with paper?

Most constructions can move to plastic-free or reduced-plastic designs. Paper windows can replace standard PET windows. Paperboard and molded-pulp trays replace thermoformed PET inserts in most cases. Deep-draw blister shapes that hold irregular forms are harder to replace directly - tray-and-sleeve is an option in most cases. We can advise on a structure-by-structure basis.

Which components come from partners?

Printing, finishing, and converting are in-house at our Ningbo factory. Any partner-supplied components are identified on the quote, so sourcing teams know exactly what is in-house and what is not before the program starts.

How do we qualify Sinso as a vendor?

Audit reports, ISO certificates, and individual compliance documents are available to qualified buyers on request. Factory visits are welcome. Contact hello@sinso.us to begin the process.
