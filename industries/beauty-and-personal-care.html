Industries · Vertical 03

Beauty & Personal Care — the finish matters as much as the formula

Beauty packaging lives and dies on tactility and shelf presence. We produce soft-touch laminated cartons, embossed rigid set-up boxes, and hot-foil beauty sets — in brands like Philips where the unboxing experience is inseparable from the product.

Why Sinso for Beauty

Tactile finishes are first-class operations on our line, not add-ons. Soft-touch lamination, embossing/debossing, hot-foil stamping, and spot UV are run inline or on dedicated finishing equipment. ECG color is available for the launch SKUs where the brand color is the brand.

Typical projects

  • Soft-touch laminated cartons
  • Embossed and debossed rigid set-up boxes
  • Hot-foil stamped gift sets
  • ECG-printed beauty SKUs for color-critical launches
  • Retail-ready secondary packaging (SRP)
