Beauty & Personal Care Packaging

Beauty and personal care is part of Sinso's Retail Packaging vertical. Soft-touch laminated cartons, embossed rigid set-up boxes, and hot-foil beauty sets — for brands where the unboxing experience is inseparable from the product.

Why Sinso for Beauty & Personal Care

Tactile finishes are first-class operations on our line, not add-ons. Soft-touch lamination, embossing/debossing, hot-foil stamping, and spot UV are run inline or on dedicated finishing equipment. ECG color is available for the launch SKUs where brand color is the brand. GMI-certified for Target and Walmart vendor programs.

Typical projects

  • Soft-touch laminated cartons
  • Embossed and debossed rigid set-up boxes
  • Hot-foil stamped gift sets
  • ECG-printed beauty SKUs for color-critical launches
  • Retail-ready secondary packaging (SRP)
