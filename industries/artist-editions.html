Artist & Collector Editions

Artist-edition puzzles, limited-run collectibles, and gallery-licensed prints demand color accuracy that standard CMYK can't reach. Saturated oranges shift. Deep blues flatten. Gradients band. ECG printing closes that gap, run by run.

Why Sinso for Artist & Collector Editions

7-color ECG on a Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 — CMYK plus Orange, Green, and Violet. The three added channels recover the saturated colors that fall outside standard CMYK's gamut: vivid oranges, deep blues, rich greens. G7 Master Printer calibration means the proof you sign off on is what comes off the press.
