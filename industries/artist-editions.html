Artist Editions — when the box is part of the art

Artist-edition puzzle and game packaging demands color accuracy that standard CMYK simply cannot deliver. Our ECG press reproduces the full brilliance of an artist's original — deep violets, gradient greens, saturated oranges — exactly as intended.

Why Sinso for Artist Editions

Native RGB workflow on an 8-color Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75. No CMYK base layer underneath, which means deep blues stay deep and saturated oranges stay saturated. G7 Master Printer calibration means the proof you sign off on is what comes off the press.

Typical projects

  • Artist collaboration puzzle boxes
  • Limited edition game packaging
  • Collector sets and rigid set-up boxes
  • Matte and soft-touch finished editions
  • Spot UV detail work on color-critical illustration
