What your channel requires

Retail channels don't share the same compliance checklist. Here's where the pressure lands for each.

Mass retail

Target, Walmart, Costco
Pressure Vendor compliance · Color consistency across reorders

GMI certification covers the color management, structural tolerances, and audit documentation these programs require. Same G7-calibrated ICC profile run to run, so your planogram facing matches the approved sample at every reorder.

Beauty & personal care

Cosmetics, skincare, wellness
Pressure Shelf presence · Exact color match to product

Cosmetic packaging lives or dies on the accuracy of its color — the carton's warm-beige needs to match the product inside. ECG runs these shades with a wider gamut than CMYK, and soft-touch lamination or spot UV give the tactile cues the category expects.

Gift & seasonal

Gift sets, holiday, occasion
Pressure Perceived value · Short runs · Hard deadlines

Rigid set-up boxes, magnetic closures, foil, and soft-touch lamination handled in-house. Seasonal lines have fixed windows — we build those timelines in from brief to delivery, not as an afterthought.

E-commerce & retail dual-channel

Amazon, DTC, plus brick-and-mortar
Pressure Transit integrity · Unboxing · Shelf-ready presentation

Packaging that ships direct-to-consumer needs to survive freight without a shipper. We design for transit integrity on the structural side, and for unboxing impact on the graphic side — without compromising the shelf-facing when the same SKU runs in retail.

For brand managers

Shelf impact you can prove before you commit

We run ECG proof-of-concepts on your existing artwork before you authorize a production run. See the color delta on your specific SKU — not a generic gamut comparison — so the shelf-impact conversation with your team is grounded in your actual packaging.

For packaging engineers

Dieline review and structural samples first

Send us your dieline and we'll flag structural issues — glue flap clearances, score placement, tuck tolerances — before tooling is cut. Physical samples of finishes on the actual substrates we run, not supplier swatches. Every quote breaks out board weight, coating, and compliance line by line.

Common questions

How do we qualify Sinso as a Target or Walmart vendor?

We're a GMI-certified vendor, which means our quality management and color process documentation meet the baseline both programs require. We can provide audit reports, ISO certificates (ISO 9001, ISO 14001), and facility information to your sourcing or compliance team on request. Factory visits are welcome — contact us to arrange one.

How consistent is color across reorders?

We run every press to a G7-calibrated ICC profile and measure inline throughout the run. That profile is locked to your job file, not reset between orders. The practical result: a reorder six months later hits the same density targets as the original. If you've had reorder drift with other printers, we're happy to do a comparison sample against your current approved before you commit.

Can you produce shelf-ready packaging (SRP) alongside the inner carton?

Yes. We produce corrugated display trays and PDQ units in-house using the same litho-lamination workflow as the inner carton, so the graphic treatment is consistent across both. Include your SRP spec in the brief and we'll quote them together.
