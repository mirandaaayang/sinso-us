Retail Packaging FAQ

We're moving our line from another printer. Can you match the packaging already on shelf?

Yes, and it's a common place to start. Send us current production samples along with your files. We measure them, match to the numbers, and send a sample of your artwork from our press within 7–10 days so you can set it next to what's on shelf. Once you approve it, that sample becomes your color standard, and every reorder is measured against it.

Can the carton match the color of the product inside?

Send us the product, or a swatch of it, with your artwork. Shades like warm beige, coral and plum are where CMYK tends to drift. 7-color ECG covers about 82% of Pantone within ΔE 2.5, so most product shades can be matched without a spot color. For large solid areas we may still recommend a Pantone spot, and we'll tell you up front.

When do we need to start to make holiday?

Start from your in-store or ship date and work backward: sample (7–10 days), your approval rounds, production, then ocean freight from Ningbo. Send us the date first, and we'll lay out the calendar with you before artwork is final, so seasonal timing is planned rather than squeezed.

How do we get a premium look without paying for a rigid box?

A folding carton with soft-touch lamination, foil or spot UV often reads as premium on shelf at a fraction of the cost. Rigid set-up boxes earn their price when the box is kept, the product is heavy, or you want a magnetic closure. We're happy to quote both so you can compare.

We sell online now and just landed our first retail account. What changes about our packaging?

Usually more than people expect: the retailer's packaging spec, a front panel that reads from across the aisle, hang or peg formats, case packs, and durability for store handling. We work through your retailer's requirements with you and turn them into a spec before anything is printed.

Can one supplier handle our cartons, hang cards and display trays?

Yes. Folding cartons, rigid boxes, backer, blister and header cards, inserts and display trays all run through the same measured color workflow. Your line stays consistent across formats, and you manage one vendor and one approval process.

What color standard do you print to, and how do you hold it from batch to batch?

We manage color by measurement, then confirm it by eye. Most factories judge a press run visually. We track ink volume and dot gain on press and test each paper batch before it runs, so a reorder measures like the first run. If your retail program sets a target (some require ΔE ≤ 2.8 on 75% or more of four-color areas), we build to it. If it doesn't, we set one with you: our internal standard is ΔE ≤ 2.5, followed by a visual check against your approved sample. When brands have split production across suppliers, other factories have been asked to match our output.
