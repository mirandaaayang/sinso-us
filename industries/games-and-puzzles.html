Why Sinso for Games & Puzzles

Game illustration typically has exactly the characteristics ECG is built for: vivid background fills, detailed character art, and side-by-side components that must match across a full print run. 7-color ECG on a Heidelberg Speedmaster XL 75 adds Orange, Green, and Violet to the CMYK base — recovering the saturated colors that standard CMYK shifts or flattens. G7 Master Printer calibration means the approved proof is what gets replicated on every reorder.

Typical projects

Card Games & TCGs

Color-matched decks and trading card game sets where illustration fidelity and color consistency across the full print run matter. Reorders hold the same ICC profile.

Board Game Components

Game boards, tiles, rulebooks, and inserts for illustration-heavy games where the visual design is part of the play experience.

Puzzles

We print the puzzle itself — the visual on the playing surface. ECG recovers the gradients, deep blues, and saturated tones that define a great puzzle image, whether it's a photographic landscape or an artist collaboration.

RPG & Supplement Books

Short-run rulebooks, sourcebooks, and game supplements with color-critical illustration spreads.
