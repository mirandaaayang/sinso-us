Industries · Vertical 02

Toys & Games — shelf-ready packaging that holds up to retail

From rigid gift sets to blister packs and retail display boxes — built to vendor spec for Target, Walmart, and specialty toy retailers.

Why Sinso for Toys & Games

GMI certification is already in place — that's the gating credential for Target, Walmart, and Costco vendor lists. Our factory has been producing retail-compliant folding cartons for 25 years. ECG color is available when the toy line warrants it; conventional CMYK runs on the same press when it doesn't.

Typical projects

  • Retail Toy Boxes — Four-color and ECG offset-printed folding cartons with window die-cuts, varnish coatings, and retail-compliant barcodes and UPC placement.
  • Gift Sets & Bundled Packs — Rigid gift boxes with magnetic closures, foil stamping, and custom-shaped interior trays — produced as a complete turnkey kit.
  • Display & Backer Cards — Hang-sell backer cards, blister packs, and floor-display packaging engineered to Walmart, Target, and Petco vendor requirements.
