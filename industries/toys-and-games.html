Retail Packaging — shelf presence backed by retail compliance

Toy lines, gift sets, beauty and personal care, and consumer goods going into Target, Walmart, and specialty retailers. GMI certification is already in place — no qualification process on your timeline.

Why Sinso for Retail Packaging

GMI certification is already in place — that's the gating credential for Target and Walmart vendor lists. Our factory has been producing retail-compliant folding cartons for 25 years. ECG color is available for the lines where shelf impact matters; conventional CMYK runs on the same press when it doesn't.

Typical projects

  • Retail Toy Boxes — Folding cartons with window die-cuts, varnish coatings, and retail-compliant UPC placement — built to Target and Walmart vendor spec.
  • Gift Sets & Bundled Packs — Rigid gift boxes with foil stamping and custom interior trays — produced as a complete turnkey kit.
  • Display & Backer Cards — Hang-sell backer cards, blister packs, and floor-display packaging engineered to Walmart, Target, and Petco requirements.
  • Beauty & Personal Care Cartons — Soft-touch laminated cartons, embossed rigid set-up boxes, and hot-foil beauty sets for brands where tactility is part of the product.
