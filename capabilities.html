Capabilities

Every stage of production under one roof

Sinso has operated as Ningbo Sinso Printing Co., Ltd. since 1998. Our 15,000+ sqm production floor in the Fenghua High-tech Development Zone runs prepress through binding without subcontracting any stage of the color-critical work.

Production stages

  1. 01 — Prepress. File preparation, G7-calibrated digital proofing, and CTP platemaking on a Kodak Trendsetter platesetter. ECG jobs use 7-channel ink separation profiled per substrate; CMYK runs on a parallel workflow.
  2. 02 — Printing. Three Heidelberg Speedmaster presses: XL 75 (8-color, the 7-color ECG press), CD 74 (7-color, conventional CMYK and spot), and XL 105 (5-color, large-format). Inline color measurement on every run; G7 Master Printer-certified operators sign off against the approved proof, not by eye.
  3. 03 — Coating. Varnishes, UV, laminations, and specialty coatings applied inline on the XL 75 or off-line for finishes requiring extended curing. See capability tables below for the full confirmed list.
  4. 04 — Die-cutting. Folding cartons, set-up boxes, display units, blister card backers. Tooling produced in-house; sample dies turn within the 7–10 day proof window.
  5. 05 — Binding and assembly. Gluing, foil stamping, embossing/debossing, rigid box assembly, window patching, and machine collation for card decks and booklets. Finished sheets pass through 100% automated sheet inspection before final QC sign-off against the approved sample.
