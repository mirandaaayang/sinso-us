The PPWR Deadline Is Real. Most Packaging Supply Chains Aren't Ready.

The EU's core compliance obligations hit on August 12, 2026. Enterprise procurement teams were moving months before that. Most factories weren't.

The EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation passed in 2024, with its core compliance obligations starting to apply on August 12, 2026 — and enterprise procurement teams weren't waiting. Retailers and brand owners selling into Europe began updating supplier questionnaires months ago, adding mandatory disclosure fields, and in some cases requiring FSC certification and recyclable construction as a condition of onboarding. The regulatory deadline is here. The sourcing deadline was months ago.

The pressure is familiar to anyone who has worked through a SMETA audit or an ISO 14001 renewal: the certification process takes time, and most factories don't have it until someone asks. Brands building a compliant supply chain today are finding that the short list of qualified manufacturers is very short.

Where Sinso fits

Sinso holds the certifications that answer those audit questionnaires directly. FSC Chain of Custody (BV-COC-129082) covers verified fiber sourcing. ISO 14001 documents the environmental management system behind the claims. SMETA compliance covers ethical trade and labor standards required by Sedex programs common across UK and EU retail. These are live, current, and documentable.

On materials: recyclable, plastic-free folding carton constructions are in standard production. FSC-certified substrates, molded pulp, and soy-based inks are available across formats.

The press configuration adds another layer. Our 7-color ECG workflow reproduces 80% of Pantone colors as a base process — no separate spot ink plates required. Eliminating spot runs means less makeready waste per job, fewer plate sets, and shorter setup runs. Color accuracy and reduced material waste point in the same direction, which is not usually how print gets sold.
